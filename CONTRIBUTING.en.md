# How to help the project

Thank you for wanting to help. This is written in plain words: no programming is needed.

## What we are looking for

1. **Errors in the content**: a wrong verse, an inaccurate summary, a wrong name of a vestment, a wrong order of parts, a wrong gesture or movement of the priest.
2. **Sources**: where exactly (edition, chapter, page) a line marked “to be verified” can be checked.
3. **Translations**: review of the Arabic and Chinese texts by a native speaker, especially theological terms.
4. **Missing material**: parts of the liturgy, lives of saints and explanations that are not there yet.

## How to report an error (the simplest way)

1. Open the **Issues** tab in this repository and press **New issue**.
2. In the title say briefly what it is about, for example: “Part 9 (Great Entrance): wrong movement”.
3. In the text give:
   - **where** on the site (page, number of the part, section title);
   - **what it says now**;
   - **what you think is correct**;
   - **the source**: book and page, a link to the text, or write “by the practice of our church” and name the tradition.
4. Press **Submit new issue**. The project owner will read it and reply.

If you are a priest or a theologian, please say so: your word is especially valuable to us.

## How to propose a change (for those who can)

1. Make a copy of the repository (**Fork**), make the change and send a **Pull request**.
2. In the description say what was changed and **which source** it is based on.
3. Only the project owner accepts changes.

## Rules

- **A source is required.** Do not write “this is the custom” without saying where and by whom. We mark “source checked” only when the edition and place are visible.
- **Do not copy other people’s text.** Paraphrase in your own words. Quotations are short (a few words) with the source named. Old translations free of copyright may be quoted, but check the rights of the particular edition. Modern translations of the Fathers and texts copied from websites are protected.
- **The labels stay.** Until a place is confirmed by a source it stays marked “to be verified”. Do not remove the label without a source.
- **Kinds of links.** “Quotation in the liturgy” (the words of the service repeat Scripture); “stated in Scripture itself” (the Bible itself links two passages); “traditional interpretation” (the link comes from tradition and needs a source).
- **Practice differs.** If a custom differs between churches and traditions (Russian, Greek, Antiochian), say so instead of choosing one.
- **Respect.** The project is about worship: write calmly and respectfully, without arguments about jurisdictions.
- **Personal data.** Do not publish other people’s personal data or contacts.
- **The owner decides.** The project owner accepts or rejects changes and may ask you to clarify the source.
